Solmere Air Service: seven sorties over the Cascavia Archipelago. Fly the SL-9 Kestrel of Vesper
Wing off the deck at Verdan Fields: thread training rings over Port Halvard, cut the low
gates of The Gullet, patrol Larkspire Heights, drop supplies over Cinder Shallows, fly the
Night Watch by instruments alone, and put her down on the deck with fuel to spare.
Throttle, fuel and damage make every sortie a decision; altitude, speed and heading sit on
the HUD beside a district minimap and the squadron radio.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts   # then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```
